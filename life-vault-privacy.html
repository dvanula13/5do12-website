Life Vault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026. This policy describes Life Vault version 1.0.0 (3). Earlier 0.1.0 builds did not include advertising or the support link.

Operator and contact

Life Vault is developed and operated by 5do12, Dario Vorkapic, Grundwaldweg 11, 72270 Baiersbronn, Germany. For privacy questions or support, contact lifevault@5do12.de.

Records and documents

Life Vault lets you manually record bills, warranties, contracts, insurance and other documents. Records may include names, providers, amounts, reference numbers, notes, related items, dates and copied attachments.

These records and copies are stored in Android app-private storage on your device. Life Vault has no developer-hosted database or synchronization server. The developer does not receive the contents of your vault. Local records rely on Android's app isolation and device protection; this version does not add an app-specific biometric or password lock.

Attaching a file makes a separate copy inside the app. The original is unchanged. When you remove a vault record or an attachment, the app removes unreferenced copies from its own documents directory. Original files and previously exported copies are separate and must be managed separately.

Permissions and reminders

Notification permission is optional and requested when you enable device reminders. The app uses locally scheduled Android alarms and a boot receiver to restore reminders after a restart or relevant system changes. It does not request broad storage, location, microphone or camera access. Android's system document picker provides access to files you select.

Reminder notifications may contain an item name and deadline. They use private lock-screen visibility, although your device's notification settings may affect what is shown. You can disable reminders in the app or in Android settings.

Backups, calendar files and other apps

Encrypted backups are created only when you choose to export them and select a destination. They include your records and attachments. The password is not saved by Life Vault and cannot be recovered by the developer. A person who has both the backup and its password can access its contents.

Calendar exports contain record titles and action dates in an unencrypted iCalendar file. Choose the destination and calendar app carefully if the titles are private.

If you choose a cloud-backed location in Android's file picker, the selected provider handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy. If you open an attachment with another app, that viewer receives access to the selected file. Life Vault does not control those independent services or apps.

Accounts and optional advertising

No account or developer-hosted vault is required. Your bill amounts, names, references, notes, payment history and attachment contents are not sent to the developer or included in advertising requests. Life Vault does not connect to banks, insurers or contract providers.

Version 1.0.0 includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for an optional small banner and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. The app requests Internet and network-state permissions for these services. Google is an independent recipient of advertising service data; the app has no separate developer analytics or telemetry service.

Before requesting ads, Life Vault asks whether you are 18 or older. This stores only an adult/no-ads choice locally, not a date of birth. No ad requests are made when you choose under 18/prefer not to answer or switch the support banner off. All vault features remain available. Advertising preferences are separate from your vault backup.

For adults who enable the banner, UMP checks applicable privacy requirements and displays a consent message where required. Ads are requested only when UMP reports that they may be requested. The app requests non-personalized ads and a maximum PG ad-content rating. Non-personalized advertising still involves service data processing and is not a promise of no tracking or no data collection.

As described in Google's SDK disclosure, advertising services can collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic/performance information and device or other identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID. Purposes include advertising, measurement/analytics and fraud prevention/security. Google encrypts SDK data in transit using TLS. The app does not request Android location permission or provide precise GPS location.

Use Settings > Small support banner to stop future ad requests. Where required, Settings > Ad privacy choices reopens the Google privacy form. Android's ad privacy settings also let you manage the advertising identifier. More information: Google privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps. Google's retention, international processing and controls are described in those policies.

Voluntary support through PayPal

Settings includes an optional Support 5do12 link. After an in-app explanation and your explicit tap, it opens PayPal in an external app/browser. No payment is made merely by opening the link. Review the recipient, amount and PayPal terms before confirming a payment there. Support provides no extra digital feature, badge, entitlement or removal of ads. No charitable or tax-deductible status is claimed.

PayPal processes the information you give it under its privacy policy. The operator may receive normal payment/receipt information and retain required transaction records for applicable accounting or legal obligations. Life Vault itself does not read payment details or receive a payment confirmation, and it does not upload your vault to PayPal.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them, replace the vault by restoring a backup, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app's data. Export a manual backup before changing devices or uninstalling if you want to keep the vault.

Exported backups, original documents, calendar files and copies held by other apps or providers are independent files. Remove them separately from each destination when needed. There is no developer-held account or remote vault to delete. The last successful backup-export date is saved locally as a reminder; Life Vault does not check whether an exported file still exists. Advertising choices and Google consent data may also remain on the device until cleared. Google and PayPal separately retain service or transaction information under their policies; clearing Life Vault does not delete records they independently hold.

Privacy requests

Contact the operator above for privacy questions or requests. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and complaint to a competent supervisory authority. For advertising and payment service records held independently by Google or PayPal, their privacy controls and request channels also apply. Local vault contents are not available to the operator.

Changes

Future versions may introduce new features. The policy and store declarations should be updated before introducing any new data collection, sharing, advertising or online services.
