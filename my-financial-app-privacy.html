My Financial App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026. Advertising and PayPal sections apply to version 3.8.0 and later. Earlier versions did not have active in-app advertising.

1. Responsible developer and contact

My Financial App is operated by Dario Vorkapic, trading as 5do12, Grundwaldweg 11, 72270 Baiersbronn, Germany. Contact: myfinancialapp@5do12.de. You can also reach us at republikaborovo1@gmail.com.

2. Your financial records remain on your device

The app is a personal record-keeping tool for manually entered accounts, balances, income, expenses, transfers, reserved money, budgets, savings, investments, debts, insurance, property and reports. These records are stored in the local app database. There is no app registration, bank connection or developer-hosted financial synchronization. The app does not send the contents of your financial records to the developer, PayPal or advertising services, and we do not sell those records. User-entered names, descriptions and notes are not included in ad requests. Language, appearance and temporary banner-pause preferences are also stored locally. Changing language changes presentation, not stored amounts or accounting currency.

3. Storage, backup and deletion

The backup password protects exported encrypted backup files. It does not itself encrypt or lock the local app database. Protect your phone using Android screen-lock and device-security controls. Exported backups use AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your chosen password. We do not receive that password and cannot recover it. You choose when and where to export a backup. A destination you choose, such as cloud storage or a sharing app, processes the file under its own terms. Version 3.8.0 configures Android to exclude app data from automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Use the app’s encrypted backup and restore feature to move records. Local records remain until you delete or replace them, clear the app’s Android storage, or uninstall the app. Exported copies remain separate. Delete them from every destination where you stored or shared them. Because we do not hold your local database, we cannot recover or remotely erase it.

4. Google AdMob advertising

Version 3.8.0 uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). A small banner can appear on the dashboard. You may choose a rewarded ad in the support panel to pause banner ads for 30 minutes. Skipping an ad does not block any financial feature. The app does not automatically display full-screen advertisements. The consent service contacts Google to determine which privacy choices apply. Ad requests are made only when UMP reports that they are permitted. Depending on your choices, region and availability, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. The financial tools remain usable offline and when ads are unavailable.

5. Advertising data and choices

Google’s advertising and consent services may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID or app-set ID, app and ad interactions and diagnostic and performance information. Google describes purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google may share this information with its service providers and advertising technology providers identified in the applicable privacy message. These service transmissions use encrypted connections. The financial records you enter are separate from this advertising data. Where required, the app displays UMP’s consent message. When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can revisit your choices under Advertising privacy choices in Settings or the in-app privacy screen. You can also use Android’s advertising-ID controls. Refusing or withdrawing advertising consent does not remove financial features. See Google’s privacy policy, advertising information and the SDK disclosure for Google’s handling, retention, international transfers and controls.

6. Optional PayPal tips

The optional PayPal button opens outside the app. You enter payment information with PayPal. The app does not receive payment credentials, add the tip to your records, remove ads in exchange for tips, or unlock paid digital features. PayPal and the recipient may process payment and receipt details to handle the tip, prevent fraud and meet applicable accounting obligations. PayPal’s privacy information applies to its independent services. A tip is voluntary support. No tax-deductible charitable status is claimed.

7. Other services, support and legal bases

Android’s document picker is used when you choose to import or export a file. Google Play and Android may independently process technical information associated with installation, updates or operation. The website privacy notice describes hosting for this page. We do not run a separate financial-data server or add a separate in-app analytics service beyond the advertising SDK described above. If you email us, we process your email address and the message you choose to send to answer your request. Avoid sending unnecessary financial records.

Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide requested app or support functions or handle a tip relies on Article 6(1)(b), applicable record-keeping obligations on Article 6(1)(c), and consent-dependent advertising and device access on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) and applicable device-access law. Necessary service security and handling general correspondence may rely on Article 6(1)(f) for the interests of operating the service securely and responding to requests. The applicable provider notices and consent message explain the providers’ additional processing and legal bases. Advertising providers may process data outside your country, including the United States. Google’s policy describes applicable transfer safeguards and entities.

8. Retention and your rights

You control retention of locally stored records and exported backups as described above. We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, or meet an applicable legal obligation. Payment receipts may have to be retained for mandatory accounting periods. Google and PayPal apply their own retention policies to data they process. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent with effect for the future. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing. Contact the developer above about app-related requests. For data a provider controls independently, you may also contact that provider. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg.

9. Audience and financial limitations

My Financial App is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children. It does not initiate bank transactions, trade assets or provide personalized financial or investment advice. Its reports depend on the records you enter.

10. Changes

We update this notice when the app’s features or data practices change and publish the revised effective date on this page.
