Orbit Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

5do12 publishes the Orbit Merge Android game. This policy explains how the game and its advertising services handle information.

Game progress and settings

Orbit Merge does not require an account. Your current games, scores, planet collection, campaign progress, and sound setting are stored in the app on your device. The game does not send this game data to an Orbit Merge server or publish it to an online leaderboard. The Daily board is generated locally from the date. Android may include app data in device backup or transfer, depending on your device settings.

Optional rewarded ads

In Endless mode, you can choose to watch an ad after the board fills. If the ad is completed, the game clears three spaces. Advertising uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The closed-test build uses Google's demonstration ads. The game can be played without watching an ad.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address, which can indicate approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts these transfers in transit. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s privacy practices.

Privacy choices

Orbit Merge uses Google's consent tools where applicable. When the app shows Ad privacy choices, you can use it to review or change those choices. Your device may also let you reset or delete its advertising ID in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps game data until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. A device backup can retain a separate copy under your backup settings. Information handled by Google follows Google's own retention and privacy practices. If you contact the developer, your message and reply address are used to respond and are kept as needed to handle your request.
