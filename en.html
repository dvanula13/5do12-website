We build websites, browser-based web apps, Android apps and Windows software. Tell us what you have in mind and what you need it to do, and we can discuss the project with you.

Tell us about your project ↗

Is My Financial App free?+

Yes. The current financial features are free and work offline. The Android app can show banner ads when online. Watching an optional rewarded ad can pause banners for 30 minutes; watching an ad or sending support is not required to use the financial features.

There is no app subscription or automatic recurring app charge in the current version.

Where are my financial records stored?+

On your device. There is no developer account or developer-operated cloud sync for your financial records. You can export a password-protected encrypted backup and choose where to save it.

Keep a usable backup before changing devices or uninstalling. We don’t receive your backup password and cannot recover it.

Does the app connect to my bank?+

No. You enter records manually. The app does not connect to your bank, execute transfers, buy or sell investments, or provide personalised financial advice.

What does supporting 5do12 give me?+

It helps the independent development of My Financial App and future free apps. Support is voluntary: it does not buy features, remove advertising or give extra benefits. PayPal shows the recipient and payment details before you confirm.

Can I help without sending money?+

Absolutely. Try the app, tell someone who might find it useful, or send thoughtful feedback. A clear bug report or an idea from everyday use can help shape what gets better next.
