Gravity Flip Rooms Privacy Policy

Publisher: 5do12 · Effective 13 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0 closed test

Gravity Flip Rooms is an offline puzzle game. This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics or real-money purchases. The game does not collect or send personal information to the publisher or to third-party services.

Progress saved on your device

The game stores your room progress, scores, earned currencies, cosmetic choices, trophies and settings on your device so they remain available when you reopen it. This data is not uploaded to our servers. Cloud backup and device transfer of game data are disabled in this version.

Sharing a result

If you choose Share, the game offers text describing your game result to your device's sharing feature or clipboard. You decide whether and where to share it. Any app you choose to share through handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Permissions and third-party software

The app includes an Android WebView wrapper and locally bundled fonts. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or advertising identifier access. Its Internet permission supports the wrapper, but gameplay content is bundled and the game contains no remote data service. Google Play and Android may independently handle store or operating-system diagnostics under their own terms.

Children

This version does not collect personal information from players of any age and includes no chat, user profiles or targeted ads.

Removing your data

Use Settings → Reset all progress to delete the game's saved progress and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. A result you separately shared to another app is managed by that app.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact 5do12 through the developer email listed on the Gravity Flip Rooms Google Play page. If this policy changes, its effective date will be updated. Any future advertising, analytics or purchase features will require an updated policy before release.
