Pocket Avalanche Privacy Policy

22 November 2026 · Android app · 5do12

5do12 publishes Pocket Avalanche. For privacy questions or requests, email republikaborovo@gmail.com. Publisher details are available in the 5do12 legal notice.

Playing and saving

No account is required. The game saves scores, settings, achievements and unfinished runs on your device. 5do12 does not operate a cloud save, online leaderboard or separate analytics service for this game. Android app backup is disabled. Your saved game stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads may appear between completed runs. You can choose a rewarded ad to continue a Classic run. Closed testing uses Google's demonstration ads. When the SDK is active, Google may process and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or account identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google's SDK encrypts data in transit with TLS.

In Settings, open Privacy choices to review the options Google makes available in your region. You can also reset or delete the Android advertising identifier in your device settings. The core game works when ads are unavailable. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps for more about Google's processing and controls.

Retention and deletion

Clearing Pocket Avalanche's storage removes your local game data. There is no 5do12 game account to delete. Google manages its own advertising records. Contact 5do12 about information you have sent us. If you email us, we use your message and reply address to answer your request and retain correspondence as long as needed to handle it or meet legal obligations. Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority.
