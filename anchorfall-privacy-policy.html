Anchorfall Privacy Policy

Who operates the game

Anchorfall is developed and operated by Dario Vorkapic, trading as 5do12, Germany. For support or privacy questions, email republikaborovo@gmail.com. Publisher contact information is also available in the 5do12 imprint.

Game data stays on your device

Anchorfall stores your current run, scores, daily results, challenge stars, achievements, unlocked themes, sound and haptic preferences, and animation settings locally in the app. It also stores an age-group choice and advertising/privacy settings locally. We do not ask for a date of birth.

The game has no player accounts, cloud saves, social features, player messaging, in-app purchases, or separate publisher analytics service. Your board contents and saved game progress are not uploaded to a publisher server. Progress does not sync between devices.

Advertising and privacy choices

Anchorfall includes Google's Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads may include menu banners, occasional full-screen ads between completed runs, and an optional rewarded rescue in Endless mode. The game is intended for ages 13 and older. Players who choose Under 16 receive no ads; the app does not initialize its advertising or consent SDKs for that choice. Players aged 16–17 receive Google's teen treatment with ad content capped at PG.

For eligible players, the app requests updated consent information and shows Google's privacy form where required before advertising starts. Where available, Settings → Ad privacy choices reopens Google's privacy options. Privacy choices and applicable legal requirements determine the ads Google can provide. Core gameplay remains available when ads cannot load or permission to request ads is unavailable.

Information Google may process

As described in Google's SDK disclosure, advertising services may collect and share:

  • IP addresses, which can be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as an advertising ID or app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement/analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS. Anchorfall does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or Android location permissions and disables the advertising SDK's coarse-location collection setting. This does not prevent Google from receiving an IP address through ordinary advertising network requests.

Google handles its advertising-service records under its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps, and Google's ad controls. Google may process data internationally and retain advertising/security records under its own retention practices.

Permissions and offline play

The app uses internet and network-state permissions for advertising and privacy services. The game content is bundled in the app and core gameplay works offline. Google advertising components may use the advertising identifier where permitted. Android's ad privacy settings let you reset or delete that identifier. The app does not request your GPS location.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear Anchorfall's app storage or uninstall the game. Automated Android app backup is disabled in this build. Copies made separately by a device, backup tool, or operating system are controlled by those services. There is no publisher-hosted player account to delete.

Clearing the app does not independently delete advertising records held by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for those records. If you contact support, your message and reply address are used to respond and are retained only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, signing keys, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive data.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information held by 5do12. For records independently handled by Google, Google's controls and request channels also apply. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Where consent is required for advertising identifiers or related processing, advertising does not begin until Google's platform permits ad requests following the applicable privacy flow. Necessary support correspondence is handled to answer your request and maintain the security and operation of the service.

Children and age information

Anchorfall is not directed at children under 13. The age-group choice is stored locally and is not a date-of-birth collection form. The Under 16 path disables advertising initialization. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact us at the address above.

This privacy webpage

Opening this page on the 5do12 website sends technically necessary connection information to the website's hosting provider. The policy page contains no analytics, ad code, or forms. See the 5do12 website privacy notice for hosting information.

Changes

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. The date and app version above identify the policy's scope. The publisher's contact address is republikaborovo@gmail.com.
