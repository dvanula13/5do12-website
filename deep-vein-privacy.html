Deep Vein — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026. Applies to version 1.0 and later. Earlier 0.4 builds do not include ads or purchases.

Developer: 5do12. Contact: deepvein@5do12.de. You can also reach us at republikaborovo1@gmail.com.

Deep Vein has offline mining gameplay, optional rewarded advertising and optional cosmetic purchases. No developer account is required to play. The developer does not operate a player-account server, cloud-save server or separate analytics service.

Progress and backups

Campaigns and settings are stored in the app's private storage. They contain your mine, equipment, in-game credits and progress, rather than your name, email or payment details. Android may back up campaign files and settings when device backup is enabled. Google controls that service and its schedule. Reinstalling the same app or setting up a replacement phone may restore an available backup. The newest session is not guaranteed to be backed up.

You can export a campaign file using the system file picker to a location or storage provider you choose. That provider has its own privacy practices. Anyone with the exported file can read its game progress. Importing a backup preserves a local safety copy of the campaign it replaces. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local files but does not necessarily remove Google backups, purchases or exported copies. Manage those through the relevant Android or provider controls.

Optional advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform for the optional daily energy refill. No ad is shown automatically. Google's SDKs may process device or other identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Deep Vein configures ads as non-personalized, uses the under-age-of-consent request setting for all players and limits ad content to Google's G rating. It does not ask for your age or birth date. Advertising and privacy choices are also governed by Google and regional requirements. Where available, use Settings > Ad privacy options to review choices. See Google privacy and advertising.

Cosmetic purchases

Styles are optional, one-time Google Play purchases without equipment or reward advantages. Google Play processes payment and associates ownership with your Google account. The app receives product information and signed purchase data to verify, acknowledge and restore an unlock. Card details are not given to the game. Purchase tokens are not included in campaign backups. Restore purchases uses the Google account that made the purchase. A campaign file is not proof of ownership. A confirmed ownership record may be cached locally for offline play and refreshed through Google Play.

Device access and support

Network access is used for optional advertising and purchases. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or broad external-storage access. Backup export and import use the file you select. Optional support, policy and Godot-license links open another app only when selected. Those services have their own policies.

Support emails and details you choose to send are used to answer your request and investigate issues. Do not send passwords, card details or sensitive information. Correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and applicable obligations. Request deletion of support correspondence using the email addresses above. Manage Google's account, advertising, payment and backup data through Google's controls.

Google Play may process store, installation and diagnostic information under its own policies. This policy will be updated when the game's data practices change.
