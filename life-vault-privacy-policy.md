## Records and documents

Life Vault lets you manually record bills, warranties, contracts, insurance and other documents. Records may include names, providers, amounts, reference numbers, notes, related items, dates and copied attachments.

These records and copies are stored in Android app-private storage on your device. Life Vault has no developer-hosted database or synchronization server. The developer does not receive the contents of your vault. Local records rely on Android's app isolation and device protection; this version does not add an app-specific biometric or password lock.

Attaching a file makes a separate copy inside the app. The original is unchanged. When you remove a vault record or an attachment, the app removes unreferenced copies from its own documents directory. Original files and previously exported copies are separate and must be managed separately.

## Permissions and reminders

Notification permission is optional and requested when you enable device reminders. The app uses locally scheduled Android alarms and a boot receiver to restore reminders after a restart or relevant system changes. It does not request broad storage, location, microphone or camera access. Android's system document picker provides access to files you select.

Reminder notifications may contain an item name and deadline. They use private lock-screen visibility, although your device's notification settings may affect what is shown. You can disable reminders in the app or in Android settings.

## Backups, calendar files and other apps

Encrypted backups are created only when you choose to export them and select a destination. They include your records and attachments. The password is not saved by Life Vault and cannot be recovered by the developer. A person who has both the backup and its password can access its contents.

Calendar exports contain record titles and action dates in an unencrypted iCalendar file. Choose the destination and calendar app carefully if the titles are private.

If you choose a cloud-backed location in Android's file picker, the selected provider handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy. If you open an attachment with another app, that viewer receives access to the selected file. Life Vault does not control those independent services or apps.

## Accounts, advertising and analytics

Life Vault does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics or developer telemetry service. The release app does not request Android Internet permission. It does not connect to banks, insurance providers or contract providers.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them, replace the vault by restoring a backup, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app's data. Export a manual backup before changing devices or uninstalling if you want to keep the vault.

Exported backups, original documents, calendar files and copies held by other apps or providers are independent files. Remove them separately from each destination when needed. There is no developer-held account or remote vault to delete.
