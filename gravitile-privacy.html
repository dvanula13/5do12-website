Gravitile — Privacy Policy

Gravitile by 5do12. Updated 21 November 2026.

Gravitile is a free puzzle game with optional rewarded video ads for players aged 16 or over. There are no forced ads, real-money purchases, subscriptions, chat or public leaderboard.

The advertising provisions below apply to the 1.0.5 update. Closed-test version 1.0.4 does not show ads.

Optional rewarded ads (16+)

Google AdMob provides videos only when you choose a clearly labelled bonus offer. A bonus is granted only when the ad service reports that it was earned. Ads may be unavailable offline or when no suitable video is available. Normal play does not require watching them. All ad requests disable personalization, use a general-audience content filter and disable SDK device-location collection. The app does not request Android advertising-ID, location or Topics permissions.

Google's advertising and User Messaging Platform services may process IP addresses (which can indicate an approximate location), app or device information and other service identifiers, consent choices, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Eligible players' consent information is refreshed at launch. Ad requests occur only after choosing a video and when Google's consent service permits them. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing. We do not send your Google sign-in identity or game save to the ad SDK. Consent choices may be stored by Google on the device. When Google requires a privacy-choice entry point, you can reopen it from Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can continue normal play without watching videos.

Under-16 players are not offered video-based extra moves, extra lives, bonus gems or lucky-reward doubling, and do not receive free tip substitutes. Regular level rewards, daily gifts and village earnings remain available to everyone.

Local play and age choice

Progress, scores, player nickname, village, game currencies, boosters, settings and the current run are stored on this device. A nickname can be fictional. Guest play does not send this information to us. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress.

Each launch asks for an age group, not a birth date. The choice is held for that launch and is not stored in your game save. Players under 16 use local play without ad requests, Google sign-in or Firebase cloud saves. For optional ads, Google receives an age-treatment signal: teen restrictions for ages 16–17, or general treatment for adults.

Optional cloud saves (16+)

Signing in uses Google and Firebase Authentication. Google provides an email address, display name and account identifier. Firebase stores account details, and Cloud Firestore stores your game progress, nickname, scores, village, currencies, boosters, save timestamps, a random installation identifier and recovery copies of saves. These support sign-in, syncing and resolving save conflicts. Normal internet requests also expose technical connection information such as IP addresses to Google and Firebase.

Cloud data is sent using encrypted connections. Saves are stored in the Firebase project's Frankfurt region. Google and Firebase process data to provide these services. Their infrastructure may process authentication, advertising, consent and service data in other countries. We do not sell personal data. The advertising services receive the limited data described above under Google's privacy policies. Game saves are not provided for ad targeting.

Retention, choices and contact

Account data and cloud saves, including recovery copies, remain until you request deletion. Verified account-deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. Google may retain limited service and security records under its own policies.

You can play as a guest, sign out to stop account syncing, request deletion of the Gravitile account and all associated cloud saves and recovery copies, or clear local app storage. Deleting Gravitile data does not delete your Google account. Copies on other devices remain until removed there.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through Gravitile. Players under 16 have local guest play only. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy or suspected collection so we can investigate and delete associated data.

For support, privacy questions, applicable access and correction requests or complaints, contact gravitile@5do12.de or the existing privacy contact borovorepublika1@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Delete account and cloud data

To request deletion of your Gravitile account, send a Gravitile account deletion request from the Google address you used to sign in. Ask to delete your account and all associated cloud saves and recovery copies. We may verify ownership before deleting them. We complete verified requests within 30 days. Never send your password.

This request is handled by support. Opening the email link creates a draft. It does not send the message or delete anything automatically.

Deletion removes your Gravitile Firebase account, its saved progress, nickname, scores, village, currencies, boosters, installation identifiers and recovery copies. It does not delete your Google account. Sign out on your devices, and clear Android app storage if you also want to remove their local copies. Signing in again after deletion creates a new Gravitile account.
