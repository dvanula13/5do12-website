Pieceloom — Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026. This policy covers the Android game Pieceloom, package app.pieceloom.game.

Contact

Pieceloom is published by 5do12. For privacy questions and support, email pieceloom@5do12.de or contact prvi.5do12@gmail.com. Pieceloom has no player account or game server.

Game data

The app stores your best scores, threads, XP, streaks and freezes, missions, unlocked themes, achievements, settings, reminder preference, ad-spacing counters, and Remove ads entitlement in app storage on your device. The developer does not receive this game progress. Android cloud backup and device transfer of app data are excluded. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes the local copy. An in-progress board is held in memory and is not restored after Android terminates the app.

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally for around 18:00. It is off by default. Android 13 and newer requests notification permission when you turn it on. No push notification token is sent to a game server.

Advertising and privacy choices

Pieceloom uses Google AdMob. It can show occasional interstitial ads on the score recap and rewarded ads when you choose Watch ad to unpick or Watch ad to double. There are no banner ads, launch ads, or interstitials during play.

The app updates privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before starting ad requests. In regions where a message is required, Google's configured message is shown. Ad requests are made only when UMP reports they are allowed. Depending on your choices and region, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. Permission to request ads does not necessarily mean consent to personalized advertising. If UMP does not permit requests, ads are unavailable and you can continue playing.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP address (which can estimate approximate location), app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostics and performance information, and device or applicable account identifiers. The documented purposes include advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit. Google's retention and handling policies apply to its records; the developer does not operate a copy of those advertising records.

See Google's privacy policy, Google's advertising information, and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Use Ad privacy in the app when that entry is available to review your choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings and use Google's privacy controls. Resetting or deleting an advertising ID and uninstalling the game do not by themselves guarantee deletion of Google's previously collected records.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase. It removes interstitial ads. The rewarded unpick and double options remain available when you choose them. The app shows the localized price returned by Google Play. Google processes payment details; the developer does not receive card details. The app receives purchase status and acknowledgement information. Pending purchases do not unlock the entitlement.

Retention and rights

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Google handles advertising and payment records under its own policies. If you have a question or want help exercising applicable access, correction, deletion, or objection rights, email prvi.5do12@gmail.com. We can help with data we control and direct you to Google's controls for data held by Google. You may also contact your relevant supervisory authority.

Children

Pieceloom is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests ask for G-rated creatives. The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

Changes

When this policy changes, its effective date and the hosted policy will be updated. The hosted version and the in-app policy should describe the same practices.
